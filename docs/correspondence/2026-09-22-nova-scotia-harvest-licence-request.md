# Draft: Nova Scotia, reuse of published deer and moose harvest statistics

*Status: draft, not sent. The owner decides whether and when to send it.*

**To:** Wildlife Division, Department of Natural Resources and Renewables
**Subject:** Permission to reuse published harvest statistics, and a question about the 2025 edition

Hello,

North Ground is building a hunting planning tool for Canadian hunters, which
names the authority for every fact it shows and links to the source.

We would like to use two published series:

1. Deer harvest statistics, specifically the table of harvest by Deer
   Management Zone (`https://novascotia.ca/natr/hunt/deer-stats.asp`).
2. Moose harvest statistics, specifically success rates by zone
   (`https://novascotia.ca/natr/hunt/moose-stats.asp`).

Two questions.

**Permission.** These pages carry Crown copyright and no open licence, unlike
the department's bear datasets on the Nova Scotia open data portal, which are
published under the Open Government Licence – Nova Scotia. North Ground is a
commercial product, so we will not use the harvest statistics until you tell us
we may. May we reproduce them with attribution, and is there wording you want
used?

**The 2025 edition.** The zone breakdown appears in the 2022, 2023 and 2024
editions, and we could not find it in the 2025 edition, which appears to report
province-wide totals only. Was the per-zone table discontinued, or is it
published elsewhere? We ask because we would rather show nothing for a year
than carry a figure at the wrong resolution.

If the answer to the first question is no, we will record that Nova Scotia
harvest data is not available to us and show nothing.

Thank you,
North Ground
