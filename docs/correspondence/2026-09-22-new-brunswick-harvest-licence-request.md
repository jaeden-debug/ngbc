# Draft: New Brunswick, reuse of published big game harvest reports

*Status: draft, not sent. The owner decides whether and when to send it.*

**To:** Fish and Wildlife Branch, Department of Natural Resources and Energy Development
**Subject:** Permission to reuse the published Big Game Harvest Reports

Hello,

North Ground is building a hunting planning tool for Canadian hunters. It shows
the official management zone a person is in, what the regulations say, and what
the province has published about hunting there, always naming the authority.

We would like to use the Big Game Harvest Reports
(`https://www2.gnb.ca/content/gnb/en/departments/erd/fish-and-wildlife/content/go-hunting/content/big-game-reports.html`),
specifically the per-Wildlife-Management-Zone tables of registered deer and
moose harvest, and the table of moose sightings per hour hunted by zone.

We note that the Wildlife Management Zone boundaries are published under the
New Brunswick Open Government Licence, but the harvest reports themselves carry
no licence, and the provincial disclaimer states no reproduction terms.

North Ground is a commercial product, so we will not use these figures until we
have your answer:

- May we reproduce the published per-zone figures, with attribution, in a
  commercial hunting planning tool?
- Does the New Brunswick Open Government Licence extend to these reports, or is
  separate permission required?
- Is there a machine-readable version we should use instead of the PDFs?

We would carry the department's own framing with the numbers, including that
population estimates are best used as trend indicators, and we would not
present any of it as a statement about whether hunting is permitted.

If the answer is no, we will record that New Brunswick harvest data is not
available to us and show nothing.

Thank you,
North Ground
