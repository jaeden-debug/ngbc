# Draft: Environment Yukon, Game Management Areas 102 and 103

*Status: draft, not sent. The owner decides whether and when to send it.*

**To:** Geomatics Yukon / Environment Yukon, Fish and Wildlife Branch
**Subject:** Game Management Areas - 250k: standing of features 102 and 103

Hello,

North Ground is preparing to show Yukon's Game Management Subzones in a hunting
planning tool. The tool cites the Government of Yukon as the authority and links
to the official regulations. Your Open Government Licence - Yukon dataset,
*Game Management Areas - 250k*, is the source.

We found a difference we would like to resolve before showing anything:

- The dataset description states 443 Game Management Areas in 11 zones, except
  national parks.
- The GeoYukon MapServer layer
  (`GY_AdministrativeBoundaries/MapServer/7`) returns 445 features with 445
  distinct `GAME_MGMT_AREA_ID` values.
- The 2015 download `Game_Management_Areas_250k.shp.zip` holds 443. The service
  adds `102` (about 9,735 km²) and `103` (about 4,351 km²). Point queries place
  those inside Ivvavik and Vuntut National Parks. Subzones `101`, `104` and
  `105` are smaller than in the 2015 file.

Our questions:

1. Are `102` and `103` Game Management Subzones under the Wildlife Act and its
   regulations, or map features for the national parks?
2. Is the MapServer layer the current version of the subzone boundaries, and
   which order-in-council map does it reflect?
3. The item notes that it is generalized from legal boundaries. Is there a
   more precise official product we should use?

Until we hear back, North Ground will not show `102` or `103` as subzones. A
location inside them will say the answer is not known.

Thank you,
North Ground
