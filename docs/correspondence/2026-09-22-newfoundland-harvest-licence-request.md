# Draft: Newfoundland and Labrador, reuse of published big-game harvest statistics

*Status: draft, not sent. The owner decides whether and when to send it.*

**To:** Wildlife Division, Department of Fisheries, Forestry and Agriculture
**Subject:** Permission to reuse published moose and caribou management-area statistics

Hello,

North Ground is building a hunting planning tool for Canadian hunters. It shows
people the official management area they are in, what the regulations say, and
what the province has published about hunting there. It names the authority for
every fact and links to your own pages.

We would like to use these published tables:

1. The moose and caribou quota and success tables in the Hunting and Trapping
   Guide, which give, per management area, the season, resident and
   non-resident quotas and the overall success rate
   (`https://www.gov.nl.ca/hunting-trapping-guide/2026-27/`).
2. The per-Moose-Management-Area tables of winter population survey results and
   summarized hunter statistics, for example
   `MMA01_St_Anthony_Tables_20220824.pdf`.

Our question is about permission, not access. The department's disclaimer says
the province owns copyright in the material on its site and extends permission
to public and non-government organizations. It does not say whether the
material may be used in a commercial product.

North Ground is a commercial product, so we will not use these figures until
you tell us we may. Specifically:

- May we reproduce these published figures, with attribution to the department,
  in a commercial hunting planning tool?
- If so, is there attribution wording you want used?
- Is there a machine-readable version (CSV or a service) we should use instead
  of reading the PDFs and web pages?

We will show your figures as the department published them, dated, with the
survey year attached to each value, and we will not present them as a statement
about whether hunting is permitted anywhere. That remains your regulations.

If the answer is no, we will simply record that Newfoundland and Labrador data
is not available to us and show nothing.

Thank you,
North Ground
