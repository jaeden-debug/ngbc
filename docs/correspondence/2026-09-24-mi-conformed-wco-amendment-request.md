# Draft: Michigan Department of Natural Resources, conformed text of WCO Amendment No. 6 of 2026

*Status: draft, not sent. The owner decides whether and when to send it.*

**To:** Wildlife Division, Michigan Department of Natural Resources (copy: Natural Resources Commission secretariat)
**Subject:** Request for the conformed text and effective date of Wildlife Conservation Order Amendment No. 6 of 2026

Hello,

North Ground is building a hunting planning tool. It shows the official
management unit a person is standing in, what the published regulations say for
that unit on that date, and it always names the authority and links to its own
source.

This is not a licence question — your GIS terms are clear and we are grateful
for them. It is a question about one document.

**What we are trying to establish:** which deer management units are in force
for 2026.

Your open-data Deer Management Unit layer returns DMU 351 (Mideast Upper
Peninsula) and DMU 352 (Midwest Upper Peninsula), both stamped `Year = "2026"`.
Your 2026 Deer Hunting Regulations Summary states that "Deer management units
351 and 352 have been rescinded", and your news release of 15 May 2026 says the
same. We believe the layer is simply ahead of, or behind, the order.

We can find the *fact* of the rescission but not the *instrument*. The minutes
of the Natural Resources Commission meeting of 13 May 2026 record the motion
and the 6–1 vote to amend Wildlife Conservation Order Amendment No. 6 of 2026
to rescind them, and a separate motion eliminating the limited firearms deer
zone. We could not locate the conformed text of the amendment as adopted; the
signed copy published before the meeting still contains sections 3.124, 12.483
and 12.484 unstruck, and the consolidated Wildlife Conservation Order carries
amendment history only through Amendment No. 5 of 2026.

**What we are asking for:**

1. The conformed text of Wildlife Conservation Order Amendment No. 6 of 2026 as
   adopted on 13 May 2026, including the floor amendments.
2. Its **effective date**. We have not assumed one. The "Sept. 12, 2026" in the
   regulations summary appears to be the Liberty Hunt opening day rather than an
   order date, and we would rather ask than guess.
3. Whether the open-data DMU layer is expected to be updated to match, and if
   so roughly when — so that we read a future difference as a lag rather than
   as a disagreement.
4. Whether there is a published list of the deer management units in force for a
   given licence year that we should be reading instead of inferring one.

Until you answer, North Ground reports Michigan as not covered and shows no
Michigan boundaries. We would rather show nothing than show a rescinded unit to
a hunter as though it were current.

Thank you,
