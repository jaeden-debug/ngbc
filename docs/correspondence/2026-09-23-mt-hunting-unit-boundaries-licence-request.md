# Draft: Montana Fish, Wildlife & Parks, reuse of the published hunting unit boundaries

*Status: draft, not sent. The owner decides whether and when to send it.*

**To:** Montana Fish, Wildlife & Parks
**Subject:** Permission to use the published hunting district boundaries in a commercial hunting planning tool

Hello,

North Ground is building a hunting planning tool. It shows the official
management unit a person is standing in, what the published regulations say for
that unit on that date, and it always names the authority and links to its own
source. It does not replace the authority; it makes the authority's own
information easier to use in the field.

North Ground is a commercial product. That is why we are writing before using
anything, rather than afterwards.

**The dataset we are asking about:** Hunting districts (deer and elk) and upland game districts

`https://gis-mtfwp.opendata.arcgis.com/ (FWP hunting district services)`

The terms of use we found state:

> "The public is granted access to information on FWP's computer system on a
> strictly 'as is' basis."

We read that as a disclaimer of warranty — a statement about quality, not a
grant of rights — and we could not find anywhere that states what may be done
with the data. We are not treating the absence of a restriction as permission.

We should mention that our work on Montana is otherwise finished: we have
matched your hunting district and upland game boundaries against your own
service, and built and verified deer, elk, antelope and upland bird season
rules from your published regulations, with every rule carrying its citation.
None of it is shown to anyone, and none of it will be, until we have your
answer.

We are asking about two different permissions, because they are different
and a licence often addresses only one:

1. **Querying your live service.** May we query the service above from our
   product, so that a hunter's location is matched against your published
   boundaries and your unit is named back to them, with attribution and a link
   to your page?
2. **Storing a copy.** Separately — and we do not need this to proceed — may we
   store a copy of the geometry, or derivatives of it, to serve from our own
   infrastructure? We are asking so that we know where we stand; a "no" here
   does not affect question 1.

We would also like to know:

- Whether attribution wording of your choosing should accompany the data.
- Whether there is a contact or process you would prefer we use for future
  updates, so that we notice when the boundaries change.

Until you answer, North Ground reports your state as "not covered" rather than
showing your boundaries. We would rather show nothing than show your data on
terms you have not agreed to.

**What we would do with it:** match a hunter's chosen location against your
published boundaries, name your unit using your own terminology, show what your
published regulations say for that unit on that date, and link to your page as
the authority. We would not present your boundaries as a legal survey, and we
would say so on the page.

Thank you,
