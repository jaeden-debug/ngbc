# Draft: Saskatchewan, reuse of Hunter Harvest Survey results

*Status: draft, not sent. The owner decides whether and when to send it.*
*Pairs with the drafted question about the Wildlife Management Zone layer.*

**To:** Fish and Wildlife Branch, Ministry of Environment
**Subject:** Permission to reuse published Hunter Harvest Survey results

Hello,

North Ground is building a hunting planning tool for Canadian hunters. It shows
the official Wildlife Management Zone a person is in, what the regulations say,
and what the province has published about hunting there, naming the authority
each time.

We would like to use the Hunter Harvest Survey results
(`https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/parks-culture-heritage-and-sport/hunting-trapping-and-angling/hunting/hunter-harvest-survey`),
specifically the tables reported by Wildlife Management Zone for draw licences
and big game management licences.

The Government of Saskatchewan's copyright page says materials may be
reproduced for non-commercial purposes and that reproduction for commercial
purposes requires advance written permission. North Ground is a commercial
product, so we are asking for that permission:

- May we reproduce the published per-zone figures, with attribution, in a
  commercial hunting planning tool?
- If so, is there attribution wording you want used?
- Is there a machine-readable version we should use instead of the PDFs?

We would show the ministry's figures as published, with the survey year and the
ministry's own confidence bounds where it gives them, and we would not present
them as a statement about whether hunting is permitted anywhere.

We have a second, separate question about the Wildlife Management Zones map
layer, which is published under the Standard Unrestricted Use Data Licence
while its catalogue entry is marked "Not for resale". That question is in its
own letter.

If the answer is no, we will record that Saskatchewan harvest data is not
available to us and show nothing.

Thank you,
North Ground
