**Status:** DRAFT for the owner to review and send. North Ground agents do not send
correspondence. Until Saskatchewan answers, North Ground reads this layer only from
the Ministry's live service at request time and stores no copy (owner decision,

**To:** Fish and Wildlife Branch, Saskatchewan Ministry of Environment
(data custodian named on the layer: "Saskatchewan Ministry of Environment, Fish and

**Subject:** Permitted use of the Wildlife Management Zones layer (WildlifeManagement MapServer, layer 0)

---

Hello,

I operate North Ground (www.northgroundbushcraft.com), a Canadian outdoor
information service. Its Hunt tool helps hunters identify the official
management zone at a location and read the rules that apply there, always
citing the governing authority and stating that the regulation, not our map,
controls.

We would like to use the Ministry's Wildlife Management Zones layer:

- Service: https://gis.saskatchewan.ca/arcgis/rest/services/WildlifeManagement/MapServer/0
- ArcGIS Online item: "Wildlife Management Zones" (item df164d6d57ad437f82c902098d06463d)
- 83 zones; boundaries last revised March 27, 2014, per the layer description

Before we do, we want to be sure our use is permitted. The Government of
Saskatchewan Standard Unrestricted Use Data Licence v2.0 allows commercial use
and distribution with attribution. The item for this layer also carries the note
"Not for resale". We read that as a restriction on selling the data itself, but
we would rather ask than assume.

Our intended use:

1. **Point lookup.** When a hunter chooses a location in Saskatchewan, we ask
   which zone contains it and show the zone number and name, citing the Ministry.
2. **Map display.** We draw the zone boundaries on our map, with attribution and
   a statement that the 1990 boundary regulation prevails over the drawing.
3. **Optionally, a stored copy.** To make answers faster and resilient to
   outages, we would like to keep a copy of the zone polygons in our own
   database and check it regularly against your service for changes. We would
   not sell, license or otherwise make the data itself available for download.

North Ground is a commercial service (it may carry advertising or paid
features in future), but the zone data would never be sold.

Could you confirm whether uses 1–3 are permitted under the Standard
Unrestricted Use Data Licence, and whether the "Not for resale" note restricts
any of them? If some are not permitted, we will limit ourselves accordingly.

Thank you for publishing this layer as a public service.
