An Agenda Change Request (ACR) has been submitted to the Alaska Board of Game requesting to 
change 5 AAC by adding a new regulation to open a hunting season for Eurasian Collared Doves. 
With respect to the board's ACR acceptance criteria: 
(A) The department is unaware of any conservation concerns, however the effects of this exotic bird 
on Alaska's wildlife are unknown. 
(B) The department is unaware of an error in regulation . 
(C) The department is unaware of any unforeseen effects of regulat ions that should be addressed in 
a change to a regulation . 
The department is unaware of any conservation concerns, errors in regulation, and unforeseen effects of 
regulations. The proposer states Eurasian Collared Doves are an invasive species and requests that a 
hunting season be opened to prevent them from spreading. The proposer is requesting a change to the 
definition of deleterious exotic wildlife, which was included in the regularly scheduled call for proposals 
for the Statewide Cycle A and B meeting in March of2016.
