## Findings
1. **Deer/turkey zones are private-land reporting/management zones, defined only by a town map.** RCSA 26-86a-6(b)(3) delegates: zones are "designated by the commissioner and listed in the applicable annual Connecticut hunting and trapping guide". The guide lists them only as a map (p.26). There are **13** zones (1, 2, 3, 4A, 4B, 5-12). The guide text's rules name only 4A, never 4B.
2. **Zones change very little**: the antlerless-only tag restriction (2, 4A), extra antlerless (7; 11 and 12), Jan 1-31 private-land archery deer and turkey (11, 12), replacement tags (11, 12), baiting ban (1-10 all land; 11-12 state land only). All other deer/turkey dates are statewide, qualified by LAND STATUS and named state-land parcels. So most legal geography for deer/turkey is state-land vs private-land, not zones. Private land = complement of itemised public/state areas; do not infer it as "open".
3. **GIS exists and is in DEEP's own org.** Item 44a687ca... (Deer_Turkey_Management_Zones) sits in ArcGIS org FjPcSmEFuDYlIdKC, whose portal record is "Department of Energy & Environmental Protection" (urlKey CTDEEP). The same org’s "Hunting Areas" experience (linked from DEEP’s public-hunting-areas page) embeds it with Migrant_Goose_Zones, Connecticut_Open_for_Hunting_New_Schema (178 areas) and CONNECTICUT_TOWN. The earlier repo claim "owned by a PERSONAL ArcGIS account" is not supported: the item owner username is karenz, an individual member INSIDE the DEEP organisation. (Whether the person is DEEP staff is not established.) Item says the zones are "Private Land deer and turkey hunting reporting zones by town" and boundaries "approximate".
4. **Verified town composition.** 13 features; my spatial join (25 random points per town) of the zone polygons with DEEP’s town polygons gives each of 169 towns exactly one zone (only coastal slivers unassigned). Counts: 1:8, 2:12, 3:23, 4A:6, 4B:6, 5:15, 6:9, 7:23, 8:8, 9:8, 10:9, 11:23, 12:19. Results agree with the guide map labels as read. Full town lists are in the row key unit_definitions_quoted (DERIVED entry). All 169 town names exist in TIGERweb County Subdivisions (layers 22 and 8). Caveat: current TIGERweb (BAS 2026, layer 8) has planning regions (COUNTY 110-190), not counties; the 2020-vintage layer 22 has counties (needed for goose "Litchfield County"/"Hartford County").
5. **Waterfowl**: North/South zone = north/south of Interstate 95 (text). Canada goose AP/AFRP/NAP-H are defined by county plus road lines; DEEP also publishes a 169-town table with 18 towns split by road description (quoted in the row). Service Migrant_Goose_Zones has 3 polygons (AFRP, AP, NAP) and does not model the NAP-H late-season carve-out; fit to the split towns untested.
6. **Sunday hunting**: guide p.11 cites Public Act 25-138 (private land, all species, any approved implement; migratory birds never) while the fetched CGA chapter 490 s. 26-73 text still shows the older bow-only rule. Treat CGA page as stale until current statute confirmed.
7. **Contradiction/unresolved**: eRegulations 26-86a-6 (dated 02/07/2020) gives Thanksgiving-relative season dates that do not match the 2026 guide dates (e.g. guide shotgun Nov. 18 - Dec. 8). The controlling instrument for dates is unresolved.
