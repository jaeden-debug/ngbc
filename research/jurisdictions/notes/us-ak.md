## Scope
Hunting is by GMU/subunit. Regulation PDFs are one per unit. Unit-pages list "State restricted areas" (closed areas, e.g. Ketchikan Road System Closed Area: "A strip 1/4 mile wide on each side of the Tongass Hwy system including the Ward, Connell, and Harriet Hunt Lake roads is closed to taking big game."), state refuges (e.g. Mendenhall Wetlands State Game Refuge "as described in AS 16.20.034 is closed to hunting, except waterfowl"). Federal public lands: "most federal public lands are open to hunting under state regulations; however, National Parks and National Park Monuments are closed to hunting except by those eligible under National Park Service regulations"; federal subsistence regs apply on federal lands (36 CFR 242 / the book flags it). Migratory birds: 5 zones by unit lists (Southeast Units 1-4; Gulf Coast Units 5-7, 9, 10 (Unimak Is. only), 14-16; Kodiak Unit 8; Pribilof/Aleutian Unit 10 (except Unimak Is.); Northern Units 11-13, 17-26) — the booklet's map page is the graphic but the text lists are authoritative-looking and clean.

## Findings
1. The repo's gis-sources URL (`huntingmaps.gmumaps`) is dead (404). Real service and real page identified.
2. Unit definitions are verbal and hydrologic (drainages, centre lines of straits, latitude of named capes, "three miles" seaward) — not derivable into polygons without the agency's work. Use the agency polygons.
3. Alaska has overlapping regimes: state GMU rules, federal subsistence rules (same unit descriptions repeated in 36 CFR 242.26 e.g. "Unit 2. Unit 2 consists of Prince of Wales Island and all islands west of the center lines of Clarence Strait ..."), park closure, migratory bird zones defined as unit lists. Legal status on federal land needs a land-ownership layer (not established).
4. Shapefile item dated eff 7/1/2025 vs regs 2026-27 — verify whether 2026 changes exist.
