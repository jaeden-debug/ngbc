- GMS/GMZ (authority summary): "The Yukon is divided into 11 Game Management Zones, and each of these Zones includes many Game Management Subzones. Harvest of big game species is recorded on a subzone basis, so know which subzone you are in when hunting. See the map included with this booklet." [Yukon Hunting Regulations Summary 2026-2027, Definitions, printed p.69 / PDF p.73]

## Scope findings
- Big game (bear, caribou, deer, elk, goat, moose, sheep, wolf/coyote/wolverine, bison): each species row lists its own GMS ranges per zone (Summary pp.45-61, Schedule B). Example Zone 1: male moose open 1-04, 1-05, 1-12 to 1-15, 1-17 to 1-19, 1-21 to 1-72, Aug 1 to Oct 31, one; 1-01 closed; male deer 1-15, 1-17..: PHA lottery only; spring grizzly 1-39, 1-53, 1-54 closed.
- Animal-class conditions: male moose only; female caribou protected; sheep must be full curl (or male 8+ years); goats male encouraged. Bag limits are territory-wide per species (caribou: area limit plus Porcupine second animal).
- Small game and non-migratory game birds: one table, territory-wide, closed in Zone 6 and subzone 4-03; Zone 5 sharp-tailed grouse 2/6 instead of 5/15. Migratory game birds are federal (ECCC).
- Counts I can state: Summary has per-zone season charts for 11 zones (printed pp.45-61); service has 445 ids by zone: 64, 93, 20, 52, 51, 13, 36, 27, 11, 32, 46. I did not tabulate the number of season rows.
- Time-dependent items in the Summary and web page: GMS 5-17 closed to sheep Aug 1 2024 to Oct 31 2026; GMS 2-16, 2-23, 2-27, 2-28, 2-39 closed to caribou after Oct 31 (may be opened by announcement); threshold hunts' limits are posted online; Kluane sheep permit (1 in 2026-27).
- Settlement Lands (Category A/B, fee simple) need First Nation consent; separate legal context, map-based; not in the served layer.
