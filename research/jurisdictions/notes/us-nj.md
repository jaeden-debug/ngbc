## Scope (deer, as read)
- Deer hunting is regulated by Deer Management Zone (DMZ). "Regulation Set: A group of deer management zones with the same season dates and bag limits. Reg Sets are numbered 0 through 8 (most restrictive to most liberal)" (digest p.26). So a NESTED_HIERARCHY: zone -> regulation set.
- Hunter-class/weapon layered on zone: "Permit Bow, Shotgun and Muzzleloader seasons also require an antlerless zone-specific permit" (p.26); "Antler Point Restriction Zone ... Applies to Zones: 3, 9, 13, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 34, 35, 47, 63 and 67" (p.27).
- Special areas: "Special Area Zone: Military bases, Federal installations ... Includes Zones: 37, 38, 39, 53, 54, 61, 64, 66, 67, and 68." Zones 45 and 46: all seasons closed except Fall Bow/Youth Archery Day (EHD).
- Digest points users to "Hunting & Trapping Explorer at NJFishandWildlife.com/hunt" to determine "deer management zone (zone), county, township and deer management unit (unit)".
- Zone definitions are textual ("boundaries are comprised of major and minor roads, waterways and geographic formations", item description) = TEXTUAL boundaries, published as GIS.
- Species differ: separate Turkey Hunting Areas (THA, 18 features, North/Central/South), Waterfowl Hunting Zones (North, South, Coastal + 7 "Closed to Migratory Bird Hunting" areas), Black Bear Management Zones (web map exists; layer not read). Other species not established.

## Reasoning / recommendation
Pattern NAMED_MANAGEMENT_UNITS with NESTED_HIERARCHY (zone -> reg set), species-specific geography (deer DMZ vs turkey THA vs waterfowl WHZ), special areas, weapon/hunter permit layering. Authority geometry exists and is authority-owned, but the DMZ licence forbids redistribution without permission and "internal purposes" use; live read for display inside a public product is arguably not "internal". Do not store/mirror. Recommended: email gisnet@dep.nj.gov to ask for permission (owner decision); meanwhile NJDEP-published textual zone descriptions (blocked here) are the alternate DERIVED_FROM_DEFINITION route; note county/township are identified by the Explorer, so county composition may approximate nothing — do not assume. Resolve the 2020-edition vs 2025-26 zone-list mismatch before any certification. Obtain the other digest segments via a permitted route (browser-rendered public access) for turkey/bear/small-game scope.
