## Scope
Big game (deer, elk, bear, cougar, turkey etc.): by GMU (3-digit numbers 101-699). Special hunts: separate overlay polygon sets (Deer Areas DA, Elk Areas EA, Moose Units MU, Mountain Goat MGU, Bighorn Sheep BSU). Migratory waterfowl: statewide ducks, but geese by Goose Management Areas 1-5 (counties + Hwy/I-5 cut) with eastern/western split; Columbia/Snake river closure strips (quarter-mile buffers); Game Reserves closed to all hunting (WAC 220-411).

## Findings that matter
1. WA has TWO complementary, both official, sources: a published polygon service AND legal text; the GIS is explicitly "generalized" and defers to the legal description. Boundary-edge uncertainty is real and the authority says so — fits the product's boundary-warning rule.
2. The service cites decodified WAC numbers (232-28-33x); controlling text is now WAC 220-410. Any provenance record must cite 220-410.
3. Goose areas are NOT in the GIS but are composable from counties plus an I-5 / Hwy 101 cut (a text carve-out: Snohomish west of I-5; Grays Harbor east/west of Hwy 101) — needs a derived line or a documented approximation; otherwise leave Snohomish and Grays Harbor point answers UNKNOWN near the cut.
4. The pamphlet PDF fetched is the Spanish edition; English twin URL not established.
