## Scope (authority's words)
Deer = four named Deer Management Units defined textually, mixing roads and whole counties:
- "Delta Unit: Areas west of I-55 and north of I-20 plus areas south of I-20 and west of U.S. Highway 61."
- "North Central Unit: All private and open public lands in Alcorn, Benton, Desoto, Marshall, Tate, and Tippah counties."
- "Southeast Unit: Areas south of U.S. Hwy 84 and east of MS Hwy. 35."
- "Hills: All areas except the Delta, North Central, and Southeast Deer Management Units." (residual)
Unit rules differ: legal-buck antler (Delta 12" spread OR 15" beam; Hills/Southeast 10"/13"; North Central any hardened antler), bag limits (NC 4 bucks, 10 private-land antlerless; Southeast 3 antlerless, 1/day), season dates (Southeast archery Oct 15 vs Oct 1; Feb 1-15 only Southeast). Overlay by land type inside units: private land vs "open public lands" vs "authorized state and federal lands" vs "Holly Springs NF (excluding portions in Yalobusha County)"; youth (15 and under) class. WMAs have their own brochures/seasons (Leaf River WMA: legal bucks 12"/15" and different dates, "Rule 1.1 BASIC REGULATIONS FOR ALL WMAs") — special-area override. Roebuck Lake (Leflore Co.) closed to Canada goose.
Turkey, small game: statewide table (no unit geography), except non-resident public-land rule (land status, "before April 1"). Waterfowl/doves: statewide dates; public waterfowl areas "may be different".
Overall: SPECIES_SPECIFIC (deer only has sub-state units).
